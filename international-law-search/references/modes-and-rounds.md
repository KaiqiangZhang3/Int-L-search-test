# Modes and Rounds Compatibility Router

This file preserves version-2 links. New projects do not use a fixed Stage 0–4 progression.

- Read [`readiness-and-scale.md`](readiness-and-scale.md) for one-question-at-a-time intake, readiness, research scale, hard deadlines, modes, and upgrade expectations.
- Read [`research-rounds.md`](research-rounds.md) for repeatable main rounds, side rounds, three-axis budgets, approval, pauses, and next-round menus.
- Read [`knowledge-and-synthesis.md`](knowledge-and-synthesis.md) for source and claim write-back, revision history, and living synthesis.
- Read [`deliverables.md`](deliverables.md) for immutable round reports, reader products, HTML, CSV, and closure artifacts.
- Read [`access-and-privacy.md`](access-and-privacy.md) before local inspection, acquisition attempts, or external retrieval.

Quick, Standard interactive, and Deep-audit remain upgradeable modes. Reuse the existing source ledger and project-level decisions across sessions; an upgrade must not restart completed retrieval or overstate earlier review. Use the minimum resumable handoff in `templates/session-handoff.md` when no durable v3 workspace is available.

Under a hard deadline, use a single compact scope card and expose the tradeoff among candidate discovery, full-text acquisition, and substantive review. Quick-mode scope approval authorizes its bounded delivery. Standard and Deep-audit projects pause after each approved round with concrete choices and require user authorization before another retrieval round or final closure. “Stop and deliver” closes the current checkpoint with accurate limits.

Every substantive language remains an independently designed branch under `multilingual-retrieval.md`, with its own purpose, vocabulary, platforms, access assumptions, three-axis budget, actual contribution, and remaining gap.

Legacy breadth and depth checkpoints may be migrated as main-round records. Their historical labels do not constrain what round type the user selects next.
