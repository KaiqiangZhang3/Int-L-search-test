# Decision Panel Compatibility Template

For new projects use [`next-round-menu.md`](next-round-menu.md). For an earlier project, show only relevant options and make the affected branch, seed, round type, or budget explicit.

- **Continue an approved branch:** choose the branch and next bounded budget.
- **Delete a branch:** remove it from future retrieval while retaining its prior record.
- **Select a seed for tracing:** identify the seed and maximum depth or source budget.
- **Review without tracing:** inspect the selected source but do not follow its citations or citing works.
- **Change a constraint:** revise a language, date range, source type, platform, candidate, or full-text budget.
- **Upgrade mode:** reuse the existing source ledger to move from quick to standard, or from standard to deep-audit, without restarting retrieval.
- **Request another bounded exploratory round:** state what uncertainty the round should resolve and approve its budget.
- **Run another breadth round:** expand an approved source type, language, region, period, or platform without assuming a depth transition.
- **Start a side round:** verify one writing-time proposition without advancing the main-round counter.
- **Synthesize without retrieval:** regenerate the current synthesis from validated sources and claims.
- **Stop and deliver:** close now and label coverage and remaining gaps accurately.
